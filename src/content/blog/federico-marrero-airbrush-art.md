Just a small quote from *[DEXTER](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0773262/)* at the start:

> She isn’t drawn to my darkness like Lila, or blind to it like Rita, and she doesn’t need it like Lumen. She accepts both sides of me; the whole Dexter. So why am I walking away from this? Because when some chemicals mix they combust and explode.

Hey, my name is **Federico Marrero** and I live in Dominican Republic.

I got my first airbrush in 1993 and since then I love everything that is in any way related to airbrush art. For some while when I stayed at home I started to make videos about everything I was doing with my airbrush, practically all the process of my works.

Alright, let's look at some of my videos. Why have I started with DEXTER quote?

## Airbrushed DEXTER

[![dexter-Federico-Marrero](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/dexter-Federico-Marrero-660x967.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/dexter-Federico-Marrero.jpg)

I made this video about Dexter because I am a big fan and I saw all the seasons of the series and I think it's really good.

http://www.youtube.com/embed/Ga3ibs6-lZw

The tools that I used here was [Harder&Steenbeck Infinity](http://www.airbrushdoc.com/airbrush-reviews/harder-and-steenbeck-infinity-review/) airbrush, paper A3 Canson 370 g/m2.

The paint that i used was water base colors (**Wicked from CREATEX**).

And I've also used some masking paper and knife.
